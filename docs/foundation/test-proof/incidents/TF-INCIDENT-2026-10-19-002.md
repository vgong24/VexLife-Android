## Source correction

The dependency-health question remains valid, but the health task must not ask Gradle to reconfigure
its task graph while a task is already executing.

The correction keeps:
- canonical task existence checks;
- repository-entry/source discoverability checks;
- the requirement that `vexVerifyJvm` includes `vexFoundationHealth`;
- the requirement that `vexVerifyLocal` includes `vexVerifyJvm`.

It changes only **how** those two graph relationships are checked: health now validates their owning
declarations in `gradle/test-foundation.gradle.kts` rather than dynamically resolving another task's
dependency graph from `doLast`.
